# mg-dangerous-delivery

Drive a loaded truck up mountain roads with no rails, through zone weather and falling rock, and deliver it. Levels are routes; harder trucks and worse weather pay more; money buys trucks and upgrades. A player arrives on foot in the lot, where the trucks are on show and sold; in a truck they are their truck (dot-vehicle's wheeled chassis, bound without the seat handover).

Read the family [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) first, and dot-vehicle's docs before touching how a truck drives. This file is only what is specific to this game. **The detail is in [docs/](docs/README.md).**

## Running and testing

- `./game.sh` plays offline; `./game.sh online` runs a local server and browser client; `./game.sh test` parses and runs the four suites (`handling` measures every truck and load; `-- --only=<word>` for one section).
- **Per-mountain settings**: `cfg/game/mg-dangerous-delivery/dot_map/map/<mountain id>.yml` (all values commented in the starters) over the whitelist in `DdModule._map_config_keys()` (weather, `mp_forceview`, `bot_difficulty`); the next mountain gets the old values back. No BSP maps here, so no converter output; `examples/headless_mapcfg` checks it. The client reads the replicated cvars, so it applies nothing itself.
- **Config goes through dot-game's `DotGameConfigBinder`** (`DdGame.config_binder`, prefix `dd`, made in `DdGame._ready`). Every `DdConfig` field is a `dd_<field>` cvar, a key in one of three grouped files, `cfg/game/mg-dangerous-delivery/config_trips|config_world|config_players/game.yml` (`DdConfig.CFG_FILES` maps each `@export_group` to a file, `DdConfig.make_binder` is the one binder every entry point uses; a key in the wrong file is refused; shipped commented out at its default by `addons/dot_game/qa/dot_game_cfg_templates.gd`, which `headless_config` checks; operator override under `cfg/server/game/...`), env/argv and a workbench "Game settings" set per file, plus an `ambience` set over the `dd_ambient*` cvars (`DdAmbience.cvar_types`, started after the cvars are bound); unknown keys stop a dedicated or headless boot, and `dd_config_diff save` writes the edits back as a shippable yml. The hand cvars (`dd_solo`, `dd_skip`, `dd_rocks`, `dd_collide`, `dd_vote_minutes`, the `dd_weather_*` four, `dd_bots`, `sv_map_rotation_file`) stay and follow workbench edits (`DdModule.HAND_RENAMES`). Trucks and cargo are the `truck` and `cargo` profile kinds (`dd_profiles.gd`): `cfg/game/mg-dangerous-delivery/truck/<id>.yml` names only the keys it changes, on top of `user://cfg/delivery_trucks.json`; trucks already on the road keep what they were built with. Client-predicted numbers in the HELLO: the weather set (now with `weather_period_sec`) and `get_out_speed`; everything else is the server's. `DotGameWorkbench` is hosted by the module (F9 off a dedicated server, HTTP only with `workbench.enabled`, `server.admins` plus dot-game's ticket login) and by the offline client. `headless_config` checks it, including a cfg file, env and argv each reaching the live config in a fresh process.
- **Noblock**: `!block`, `!noblock`, `mp_teamblock` 0 and `mp_enemyblock` 1 from `DotGameNoblock`, `mp_solidplayers` 0 (`DdGame.body_rules`). Walkers never collide with each other and trucks collide by `trucks_collide`, so out of the box nothing physical changes; with `mp_solidplayers 1` a pair of trucks collides only when the block rules say so (everybody is one side, so `mp_teamblock 1` as well). The client follows with `platform.add_noblock`. `dedicated` checks it.
- After a route change: `tools/build_routes.py --check` and `tools/drive.sh` (also `TRUCK=semi`, `TRUCK=bulk`).
- After touching a sound or a level: `addons/dot_audio/tools/loudness.sh --no-walk --scene=res://tools/loudness.tscn [-- --sky=rain]` (the family's −17 LUFS band; on foot it is the same script with no `--scene`, and `tools/loudness.sh` is the quick local one); the levels are `DdAudio`'s named constants, a route's bed is `DdAudio.ROUTE_BED`. [design](docs/design.md#sound-is-a-dot-audio-catalogue-and-every-loop-is-shaped)
- After touching a camera, the road, the terrain or a gate: `tools/camera_check.sh` (every road, armed; `--render` for frames). [design](docs/design.md#the-cameras-stay-out-of-the-mountain-and-the-server-may-hold-one)
- **Render after touching the road, the terrain, the HUD or the lot** (`tools/shot.sh`; `--ballot` for the mountain vote, `--chat=8` for a full chat log, `--mountain=<id>` for the lot on another mountain): every mountain problem was found only in a picture. [testing](docs/testing.md)

## Rules

- **Every key is a `DdBindings` row read through its action** (driving polls the actions; the arrows drive too, through `ui_*`, which no row holds); the HUD's key list is `DdBindings.hud_lines`. H is help (dot-menu's stock key; `DdSettings._fill_help`), so no row may default to H. Escape stays the lot; the menu with Help and Leave is O and the lot's Settings button. `headless_run` checks it.
- **M is the mountain list** (dot-menu's map menu): `DdGame.mountain_rows()` and `set_mountain()` solo, the module's `_change_mountain` on a server, where dot-vote's map menu lets the owner's `sv_map_menu_flag` change it now and gives anybody else a vote. No row may default to M; `headless_run` and `dedicated` check it.
- **E is one ACT, `use`, decided by `DdGame.use_choice`** (get in, get out, buy, drive one you own), and the HUD's line is the same function on the client; buying, choosing and upgrading are the lot's alone (`in_lot` in `DdBridgeActs`). A driver has one truck: off a road it is empty and parked in a bay (`_park`), a trip replaces it, back from a road it is parked again. [world](docs/world.md#buying-and-a-truck-off-the-road)
- **On foot, the owner walks and the world only refuses** (`DdGame.walk`: step length, `walkable`, a PLACE back); nothing on foot is predicted. Others are drawn from `Driver.walker` by `DdClient._present_walkers`, never the owner's own. Stand-ins walk through `walk` too and drive the land's road to a gate unless `quick_start`. [world](docs/world.md#stand-ins-go-the-way-a-person-goes)
- **In a truck a player is their truck.** `DdTruck` binds `DotVehicleInstance` and `DdDrivetrain` (a `DotVehicleWheeled` by path) itself; `DdGame` calls `drive()` once a tick. Do not bring in `DotVehicleRide`. [design](docs/design.md)
- A route is a level and a corner of one world; a new route is a file in `routes/`. Hairpins always put the cliff on the inside and a rail round the outside, 1.5 m wider than the road. [design](docs/design.md#one-world-holds-every-route-and-a-route-is-a-level)
- **No top-speed ceiling.** `DdDrivetrain` (power, torque, gears, air, tyres, governor) decides how fast a truck goes; never cap with `top_speed`, never let a truck body keep Godot's linear damping (`tunables.linear_damp = 0`: dot-vehicle writes it onto the body at bind, over `DdTruck`'s). Godot's wheel `brake` is an impulse per step: brakes are newtons times the step, which dot-vehicle's chassis does (4f4afaf); a trailer's own brakes multiply by the step themselves. [design](docs/design.md#how-fast-a-truck-goes-is-the-truck-and-the-road-never-a-ceiling)
- A load (`DdCargo`, the route's `cargo`) is a share of `capacity_kg`, on the trailer for a truck that pulls one; it changes mass, centre of mass, wind, knocks and pay, and fuel sloshes. `handling` checks the trucks and loads differ. [design](docs/design.md#a-load-is-a-share-of-the-truck-and-it-changes-the-drive)
- **Each truck handles its own way, from `DdTrucks.DEFAULTS`**: `friction_slip` near a real tyre's (1.05 to 1.3; it was 2.6 to 3.2 and every truck but the Bulk spun at full lock at 55 km/h), `rear_grip_fraction` above 1 so the back holds and a truck washes wide instead of swapping ends, a load on the bed taking grip off the front against the back (`tunables_for`), and its own `suspension` (the Box Truck softest, so it leans most). `tools/handling_probe.tscn` prints each truck's cornering g, roll, full-lock slip and stop from 60 km/h; tune by it.
- **What comes off is dot-vehicle's `DotVehicleDamage` / `DotVehicleBreakage` / `DotVehicleDebris`; the game only declares it.** `DdTrucks.PARTS` (regions as fractions of the body box, strength in m/s at the part, times a truck's `toughness`) are carved off the one-piece Kenney body at load; the load comes in `CARGO_UNITS` units whose loss is a share of the pay and of the mass; `tyre_strength` / `hub_strength` per truck. `DdGame.truck_damaged` carries each loss; the bridge sends the whole state as `DAMAGE` (and `damage` in a joiner's DRIVER body) and a client's mirror (`breakage.simulate = false`) throws its own pieces, which touch only the world (camera layer), never a truck. Effects (`effects/*.tscn`, CPU particles) are the client's, from `truck_damaged` and `truck_scraping`. `tools/damage_shot.sh` renders a battered truck; `handling --only=damage` checks it. Hits obey `harm_gate`.
- Every shipped route keeps five kinds of challenge or more, traffic among them, never 240 m apart (`handling`). Oncoming cars run on the tick's schedule and stop for a truck in their lane; nothing about them is sent. [design](docs/design.md#every-route-has-challenges-a-few-hundred-metres-apart-at-most)
- A truck put down is held `SETTLE_SECONDS` on its brakes; one hung up (wheels off, still) for `hung_respawn_seconds` is put back like a fall. [design](docs/design.md#put-down-a-truck-is-held-and-one-hung-up-is-put-back)
- Every Label3D on the road is one-sided and faces +Z (back toward the driver); a gate has one per face. `handling` checks. The pointer is `DdClient.pointer_mode`'s, every frame (dot-menu's `manage_pointer` is off). [design](docs/design.md#signs-read-from-the-driving-side-and-the-pointer-is-out-of-the-way)
- **A map is a mountain: a document of routes and their layout** (`DdMapDoc`, `maps/`, a pack's `routes/` by `kind`), sent to clients with the routes (HELLO, MOUNTAIN), never anything built from it. `library` is every route loaded, `documents` the map's, `maps` the catalogue; only `set_mountain` changes it, and it takes every truck away first. Every shipped map must build with no road crossing (`headless_run`). [world](docs/world.md#a-map-is-a-document) The vote is TIME_LIMIT on dot-vote's clock (no rounds), its change waits for people mid-haul (`note_road_clear`), and every change goes through `DdModule._change_mountain` so the clock and the world agree. [design](docs/design.md#a-mountain-is-a-set-of-routes-and-the-players-vote-for-the-next-one)
- **maps.yml** (`cfg/addons/dot_map/maps.yml`, the game's `cfg/game/mg-dangerous-delivery/dot_map/maps.yml`, then the operator's `cfg/server/addons/dot_map/` and `cfg/server/game/mg-dangerous-delivery/dot_map/`; an operator file replaces the shipped one unless it starts `merge: true`; the flat `cfg/maps.yml` works until 2026-12-31; `sv_map_rotation_file`): dot-map's `DotMapRotationFile` decides which mountains ROTATE. `DdGame.rotation_mountains()` mirrors a `DotMapDef` per mountain, applies the file and returns the mountains the active rotation plays (a `cycle` in its written order, starting after the mountain in play). The vote's ballot and nominations read it; `dd_mountain <id>`, `set_mountain` and the M menu still reach an unlisted mountain, and the list and menu mark it "(not in rotation)". `commands:` renames dot-vote's roles (`commands_spec_fn`) and `dd_mountain`; `dd_rotation [name]` switches, `dd_maps_reload` re-reads. There is no timed draw of the next mountain (only the vote and an admin change it). No file, or one matching nothing installed: every mountain. The source is handed `in_rotation_fn` and `rotation_order_fn` (dot-vote API 12) so merged Workshop mountains follow the file too. `dedicated` holds it.
- **Stage hazards** (natural-disasters' tornado, wind gusts, quake) come from `stage_hazards.yml` per route, are set off by a truck nearing a seed-fixed site with a siren and HUD warning first, and stay small enough to finish through (`headless_run` measures). Numbers go in the yml, never in `dd_disasters.gd`. [design](docs/design.md)
- **Never Godot's `hash()` for a roll**: use `DdWeather.unit()` (MD5). Weather, hazards, boulders and terrain noise must be the same on every machine. [design](docs/design.md#weather-is-the-worlds-and-a-trip-is-paid-for-what-it-met)
- Pay counts only the weather and rock a trip met (`DdTrip.meet`). Ice and debris are road, laid from the document; debris never takes both lanes.
- Falls are a height test, never a trigger volume; R costs what a fall costs; a respawned truck is a ghost until clear of every other truck; rock is rate-limited per site. [design](docs/design.md#falls-are-a-height-test-and-rock-is-rate-limited)
- Solo is on K; B is the family's loadout key and nothing here may default to it (`headless_run` checks). A player whose settings file already stored B keeps it.
- Solo is collision exceptions (`_refresh_exceptions`, both ways, recomputed on every change) plus visibility (`sees`); it does not touch chat.
- The chase and high cameras are swept against `DdRoute.CAMERA_LAYER` (the mountain, gates, road, debris); anything a camera must not enter goes on that layer, never on layer 1. [design](docs/design.md#the-cameras-stay-out-of-the-mountain-and-the-server-may-hold-one)
- Every teleport re-hitches a trailer (`DdTrailer.rehitch`); a trailer shares its truck's collision exceptions.
- **A route is entered at its gate, never past it**: the barrier stops a truck, E at it accepts (`DdGame.accept`, the trip's own truck put down past the barrier), a people-only wall and `walkable` stop walkers; SET OFF drives to the gate unless `quick_start`. The land (`DdLand`) collides, an island's mountain never does, and each mountain is held under the land's roads (`finish_terrain`). [world](docs/world.md#the-land-a-road-to-every-route-and-a-gate-on-each)
- **The road's trimesh is the only floor** of a route. The mountain collides with nothing and is held under the road by `BAND` (8 m, more than a cell's diagonal); `headless_run` checks every half metre of every road. [terrain](docs/terrain.md)
- `PackedVector3Array` is copy-on-write; Godot's front face is clockwise. [terrain](docs/terrain.md#the-road)
- **Nothing is predicted.** The drive is four bytes a tick; bodies ride snapshots; everything else is JSON; HELLO carries what a client needs to compute weather and roads itself. [netcode](docs/netcode.md)
- **A READY before the player is seated is kept** (`_ready_unseated` in the bridge) and answered by `add_player`: over ENet with loss it overtakes the link's `loaded`, and a dropped READY left the client waiting for ever. `headless_net` sends it first (armed). [history](docs/history.md)
- Every garage button and key is one ACT through `DdBridgeActs.run`, which offline calls directly; never a second code path.
- Money is keyed by the session's account uid, not the platform profile (which arrives after seating). **One account, one driver: the newest connection wins** (two tabs of a guest share a device id); the older is taken out and kicked with "You joined from another tab or window". [netcode](docs/netcode.md#one-account-one-connection)
- Settings open from the lot's button and O, never Escape (Escape is the way back to the lot).
- The lot's toys are loose dot-props toys (`DdGame.TOY_SPOTS`) on their own bit: trucks plough them, a walker's step pushes them (`_advance_toys`); a client registers every toy mirror on TOYS before its lot is built. [world](docs/world.md#the-lots-toys)
- The HUD follows the menu's theme and HUD size (`DdHud.follow_menu`, after `DotGameClient.setup()`, which also calls `follow_hud` on it); `DdHud.piece_theme()` is the accent only, plates restyle from the theme's `plate`, and no Label sets a colour except the accent lines. `headless_run` checks the default and a theme change.
- The lot moves left of any visible `dot_hud_cover` Control over it (the shell's ballots); read the group by its string, never a dot-ui constant. [design](docs/design.md#the-lot-makes-room-for-a-ballot)
- The lot also lifts and shortens (to `LOT_MIN_HEIGHT`) above the rows the chat log is drawing (`DdGarage.chat_window`); a full log lay over the routes column. `headless_run`'s lot section checks it. [design](docs/design.md#the-lot-makes-room-for-a-ballot)
- A sound or shake the server's world causes reaches a connected client as an event (appended to `DdEvents.Kind`) re-emitted on the mirror world, where the offline handler already listens; never an offline-only `connect`. [netcode](docs/netcode.md)
- Every sound is a `DdAudio` catalogue id; a loop is shaped through `manager.shape()` (dot-audio API level 2), never by restarting it. Sound is proven only under xvfb (`tools/audio_probe`). [design](docs/design.md#sound-is-a-dot-audio-catalogue-and-every-loop-is-shaped)
- **Quiet moods under the mountain**: `DdAudio.ambient` is dot-audio's `DotAmbienceDirector` (moods by mountain id prefix, defaults in `game/dd_ambience.gd`), picked from `DdGame.map_laid` (every build of the routes, server or client); the per-route bed plays only when no mood loaded. `dd_ambient*` are bound by the module (REPLICATED, `dd_ambient_dir` local) and followed by the client; the Ambience bus follows the Effects slider. `dedicated` checks a server change reaches a client's director and the mood follows the mountain. A map document's `ambient` `{level, lively}` (kept by `DdMapDoc.normalise`) scales the bed: open high ground louder and lively (wind, 1.3 and 1.2 for the range), low country 1.0 lively, the long haul a quiet 0.8 not lively because it is hours of engine and the bed should not compete.
- The light is a daylight dot-lighting document turned down under Compatibility, dimmed by weather; the surfaces keep the mountain's colours (no pastel grid: snow and wet are read from them). [design](docs/design.md#the-light-is-the-familys-daylight-the-colours-stay-the-mountains)
- No `class_name`; every own `res://` path through `DdPaths.rebase()` where defined (see [mg-buses-from-hell's delivery.md](../mg-buses-from-hell/docs/delivery.md)).
- `project.godot` carries no comments (the editor drops them); the reasons for its settings (gravity on the world's space, 60 physics ticks, `gl_compatibility`, uncompressed texture imports) are in [design](docs/design.md#project-settings).
- **A driver behind their client's loading cover loses nothing on a server** (dot-game's "still loading"): `DdGame.harm_gate` (set by the module from `can_peer_be_harmed`, keyed by driver key through the bridge) is asked by the knock, `_fall` (the truck is still put back, but no load and no counted fall) and the lightning's `_damage`. Offline and stand-ins have no gate. `dedicated` checks it over a real socket; `tools/render_cover.sh` renders the cover and the frames after it lifts (it found the gate's stock cover left up over the lot once `DdLoadScreen` replaced it).
